Design System

The source of truth for how CasaCEO looks and feels. Every page builds from this so nothing improvises. The feeling we're after: calm, trustworthy, a place that's mine — capable software that still feels like it's about a home, not a bank.

Buttons

One primary action per view. Everything else steps down. An action keeps its name through the whole flow — "Mark as Paid" produces "Marked paid," never a surprise.

PrimarySolid navy, white text. The one main thing to do. Rounded 12px.
SecondaryWhite with navy outline + navy text. A real but lesser action.
QuietTransparent, soft border, muted text. Cancel, dismiss, "not now."
Inline linkUnderlined faint text. Low-stakes reversible actions like Undo.

Label vs. button — the difference

A label tells you a state (you can't click it). A button does something (you can). Keeping these visually distinct is what stops a page feeling confusing.

Autopay Beach House ← labels: rounded chips, muted, no shadow, not clickable
← button: solid, has affordance, clearly tappable

Wording

Words are part of the design. Plain, active, and named for what the person controls — never for how the system is built.

Plain over clever"Forward by email," not "Email ingestion." "All set," not "Reconciled."
Active & consistentA button says what happens: "Mark as Paid" → toast "Marked paid." Same word throughout.
Empty states invite"Add your first bill and never miss a due date" — a door, not a dead end.
Errors directSay what happened and what to do. No apologizing, no vagueness.
Sentence caseButtons and headings in sentence case, not Title Case Everywhere.

The through-line

Every choice passes one test: does it keep the page simple, calm, and trustworthy — or busier? Consistency reads as quality; clutter reads as a team that couldn't decide. Spend boldness in one place (the gold), keep everything around it quiet. A great 3-tile product beats a mediocre 6-module one.
